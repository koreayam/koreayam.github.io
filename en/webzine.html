Haneul Sarang

The YAM Webzine

Haneul Sarang ("Love of the Sky") is a newsletter created in 1993 to encourage exchange among Korean astronomy graduate students. After Issue 4 in 1995 publication paused, until it was revived in 2016 as a webzine starting from Issue 5. The early issues are written in Korean; click through to read.
